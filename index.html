What the model does

Isolation Forest · exploratory anomaly scoring

The browser implementation fits a small Isolation Forest separately for each station with at least 30 model-eligible records. It uses AQI and the change from the previous record. This is exploratory within-uploaded-batch ranking, not a verified-baseline/new-record pipeline. Higher scores mean “more unusual relative to this uploaded batch,” not a probability or cause.

  • Malformed, duplicate and future-dated rows are held out. Stale latest records and repeated-value cue records are excluded from model fitting and scoring. Other old observations may remain as historical batch context.
  • Repeated exact values across three or more consecutive readings receive a transparent review cue.
  • The newest observation is compared with the reviewer-selected freshness window.
  • Review-score cutoffs are not calibrated or validated; a person must inspect official sources.
No eligible official station data is bundled.
The supplied city/day workbook has no station ID, time of day, source link, or verified provenance. It is deliberately not used to train this model.

03 · Human verification

From a data cue to a school process

The AI part ends at a review cue. The school retains authority over every real-world action.

03
01 · DATA

Official station history

Reviewer obtains timestamped information from a verified source and records station, units/method where published, and source link.

02 · CHECK

Validate before scoring

Hold incomplete, duplicate, malformed, future-dated, or stale records for human checking; never fill a missing value silently.

03 · AI REVIEW CUE

Prioritize unusual records

Isolation Forest score plus simple repeated-value and freshness cues. A score is not a probability, explanation, or diagnosis.

04 · PERSON

Verify the source

A focal person checks the original reading, timestamp and context, then records valid, stale or unresolved.

05 · SCHOOL LEAD

Check current directions

Only school leadership applies current CAQM/GNCTD orders to school communications or operations. The prototype sends nothing.

04 · Assess impact & scale

What is ready—and what is not

A portfolio prototype is not evidence that a school pilot has happened.

04

Implemented here

  • Historical monthly summary from the supplied AQI workbook.
  • Local CSV parsing, required-field and duplicate checks.
  • Station-specific Isolation Forest scoring for eligible uploads.
  • Reviewer queue and temporary in-tab note log.
  • Offline use; no external service or server is required.

Needs validation before any pilot

  • Verified station-level data, source, units/method and authorized use.
  • Manual labels and evaluation of false alerts, missed issues and reviewer workload.
  • School feedback, privacy review, accessibility and a safe fallback process.
  • Current official directions and school-lead approval for any real communication.
Hard guardrail: The prototype cannot issue health advice, decide whether classes close or change mode, determine order applicability, or send an alert. No student or individual health information should be entered.
